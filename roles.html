Frequently Asked Questions


How does SAHARA's SafeMap help me find safer routes?

SafeMap uses AI-powered safety risk prediction and route optimization to analyze road segments based on streetlight coverage, CCTV availability, police proximity, and community-reported incidents. It generates Safest, Balanced, and Fastest route options so you can make informed travel decisions.

What happens when I trigger an SOS?

Your live location is immediately captured, trusted contacts are notified, and the nearest verified volunteer is matched to respond. The entire process — from volunteer acceptance to arrival to resolution — is tracked in real-time with live status updates.

How does the Volunteer verification process work?

Volunteers register through the app, submit their details for admin review, and once verified, can set their availability status. Only verified and available volunteers are matched for SOS requests. Unverified or suspended volunteers cannot accept emergency assignments.

Can the AI detect distress without me manually activating SOS?

Yes, SAHARA's Safety Fusion system combines signals from journey anomaly detection, wearable distress monitoring, and voice analysis. If multiple signals suggest possible distress, the system asks "Are you okay?" before escalating. Normal activities like running do not automatically trigger emergencies.

How do I contact support or report an issue?

You can reach our support team through the Contact page in the app, submit community safety reports, or use the in-app feedback system. For technical issues, report through the system and our team will respond promptly.
